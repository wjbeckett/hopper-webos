## Short description
Watch your own Silo media server on your LG TV.

## Description
Hopper connects your LG TV to your own Silo media server. Browse your movies, series and
audiobooks in a fast interface built for the remote. Pick up where you left off on any device.

- Continue Watching and personalised rows on Home
- Full details with cast, versions, audio and subtitle choices
- Remote-first navigation: every screen works with arrows, OK and Back
- Sign in with your phone, or with a username and password
- Multiple profiles with PIN protection

Hopper needs a Silo server that you run yourself. It does not provide, host or sell any content.
Hopper is an independent client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Silo.
